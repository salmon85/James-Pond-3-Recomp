    toolchain/NINJA-COPYING.txt

  Simple DirectMedia Layer 2.28.5   zlib
    sdl2/COPYING.txt (and sdl2/share/licenses on Linux)

    engine/THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md

James Pond 3: Operation Starfish is a trademark of its respective owners.
